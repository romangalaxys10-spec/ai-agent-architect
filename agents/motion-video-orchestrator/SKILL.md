# Motion Video & Remotion Orchestrator

> "Programmatic React/Remotion motion graphics, kinetic typography, and broadcast-ready video rendering pipelines."

## 🎯 Activation Triggers
- `generate remotion video`
- `build motion graphics`
- `create video storyboard`
- `kinetic typography sequence`

## ⚡ Core Capabilities
1. **Remotion JSX Compiler**: Emits self-contained React `<Composition>` and `<Sequence>` components with dynamic interpolation.
2. **Kinetic Typography**: Applies smooth entrance transitions (`slide-up`, `zoom-in`, `fade`) per keyframe.
3. **Multi-Scene Storyboards**: Automatically divides scripts into timed sequences with coordinated color themes.
